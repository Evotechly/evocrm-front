## Hierarchy and limitations

EvoCRM Screen requirements > Brandbook > approved Figma > Mobbin > Shoogle. The referenced Figma node says concept direction only. Brandbook Geist wins over Inter in the concept board. Contacts local search intentionally includes company as requested in F2; production currently searches only name/email. Local status is the foundation ContactStatus, not production lead status. See baseline audit and integration documentation before replacing adapters.

Settings, administration, onboarding and authentication are outside the five implemented screens. Research for those modules must be repeated against their specifications in the next wave; no completion claim is made here.
